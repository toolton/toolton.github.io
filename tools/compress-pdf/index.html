Free PDF Compressor

ToolTon PDF Compressor helps you reduce the size of PDF files directly in your browser. No account or software installation is required.

It can be useful when a website has a PDF upload size limit for applications, forms, documents, assignments or other online submissions.

How to Compress a PDF

  1. Select your PDF file.
  2. Tap the Compress PDF button.
  3. Wait while the PDF is processed.
  4. Download the resulting PDF.

Is my PDF uploaded?

No. Processing is performed inside your browser. ToolTon does not need to upload your PDF to a server for this tool.

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I compress a PDF online for free?

Select your PDF in ToolTon's PDF compressor and tap Compress PDF. The processed file can then be downloaded directly to your device.

Can I compress PDF on my phone?

Yes. ToolTon is designed to work on phones, tablets and desktop browsers.

Do I need to install software?

No. The tool runs in a modern web browser.

Is the PDF private?

The PDF is processed locally in your browser, so the tool does not require uploading the document to a server.

Will every PDF become smaller?

Not necessarily. Some PDFs are already highly optimized, so their size may change very little or may occasionally remain similar after processing.
