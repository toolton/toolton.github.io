Free PDF Compressor

ToolTon's PDF compressor lets you reduce the size of a PDF file directly from your browser. It is designed for quick use on phones, tablets and computers.

Smaller PDF files can be easier to share, store and upload when a website has a file-size limit.

How to Compress a PDF

  1. Select or drop your PDF file.
  2. Tap the Compress PDF button.
  3. Wait while the file is processed.
  4. Download the resulting PDF.

Why Compress a PDF?

Large PDF files can take longer to upload and may exceed file-size limits on application forms, document portals, email services and other websites. Compressing a PDF can make the file easier to handle.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I compress a PDF online?

Select your PDF above and tap Compress PDF. ToolTon processes the file in your browser and provides the resulting PDF for download.

Is the PDF compressor free?

Yes. ToolTon's PDF compressor is available without requiring an account.

Can I compress a PDF on a phone or tablet?

Yes. The tool is designed to work in modern mobile and desktop browsers.

Is my PDF uploaded to ToolTon?

No server upload is required by this tool. PDF processing happens in the browser.

Not necessarily. Some PDFs are already optimized, so their size may change very little or may remain similar after processing.
