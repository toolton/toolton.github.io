How to Calculate Percentages

A percentage represents a number as a fraction of 100. Use the calculators above to quickly solve common percentage problems without doing the calculation manually.

What is X% of Y?

To find X% of Y, multiply X by Y and divide the result by 100.

Formula: X% of Y = (X ÷ 100) × Y

X is what percentage of Y?

Divide the first number by the total number and multiply the result by 100.

Formula: Percentage = (X ÷ Y) × 100

How to calculate percentage increase or decrease

Subtract the original value from the new value, divide the difference by the original value, and multiply by 100.

Formula: Percentage change = ((New value − Original value) ÷ Original value) × 100

Example: 25% of 400

25 ÷ 100 × 400 = 100

Example: 50 is what % of 200?

50 ÷ 200 × 100 = 25%

Example: 80 to 120

The increase is 50%.

Example: 200 to 150

The decrease is 25%.
