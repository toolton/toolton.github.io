Free Image Resizer Online

Toolton's Image Resizer lets you resize images and photos online without installing software. Change exact pixel dimensions, resize by percentage, keep the original aspect ratio and choose JPG, PNG or WebP as the output format.

How to resize an image

Upload a JPG, PNG or WebP image. Enter the desired width and height, or choose a resize percentage. Keep Lock aspect ratio enabled when you want the image proportions to remain unchanged. Then select Resize Image and download the result.

Resize JPG, PNG and WebP images

You can resize common image formats including JPG, PNG and WebP. Choose the output format you want before downloading the resized image.

Resize an image to specific dimensions

Enter the exact width and height in pixels when you need a specific image size. With aspect ratio lock enabled, changing one dimension automatically calculates the matching dimension.

Resize an image by percentage

Quickly resize an image to 25%, 50%, 75%, 100%, 125%, 150% or 200% of its original dimensions. This is useful when you need a quick smaller or larger version of a photo.

Image Resizer FAQs

How do I resize an image online?

Upload your image, enter the desired width and height or choose a percentage, then select Resize Image. You can download the resized image immediately.

Can I resize a JPG image?

Yes. JPG images are supported. You can change their dimensions and choose the output format before downloading.

Can I resize a PNG image?

Yes. PNG images can be uploaded and resized directly in the browser.

Can I resize a WebP image?

Yes. WebP images are supported along with JPG and PNG.

How do I resize an image without changing its proportions?

Keep Lock aspect ratio enabled. When you change the width or height, the other dimension is automatically calculated from the original image proportions.

Yes. Enter the required width and height in pixels. The tool supports dimensions up to 10,000 pixels.

Can I resize an image by percentage?

Yes. You can quickly choose 25%, 50%, 75%, 100%, 125%, 150% or 200%.
