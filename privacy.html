PRIVACY POLICY

Your privacy matters.

This Privacy Policy explains how Toolton handles information when you visit our website and use our online tools.

Information we collect

Toolton does not ask you to create an account simply to use the publicly available tools on the website.

Depending on the services we use in the future, certain technical information such as browser type, device information, approximate location, or usage information may be collected automatically for security, performance, analytics, or service improvement.

Files and tool processing

Some Toolton tools may allow you to select or process files such as images or documents.

Where a tool performs processing directly in your browser, the selected file can be processed locally without being uploaded to our servers.

Not every tool necessarily works in the same way. Each tool may provide additional information about how its processing works.

Cookies and similar technologies

Toolton may use cookies or similar technologies in the future for essential functionality, analytics, preferences, security, or advertising.

If third-party services that use cookies are added, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain their use.

Third-party services

Toolton may use third-party services for functions such as website hosting, analytics, security, advertising, or other infrastructure.

These services may process certain information according to their own privacy policies.

Data security

We take reasonable steps to keep Toolton secure and to reduce unnecessary collection or transmission of information.

However, no website or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children's privacy

Toolton is not specifically directed at children, and we do not knowingly request personal information from children.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Toolton's features, tools, or third-party services change. Updates will be published on this page.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please visit our Contact page.
